METHODOLOGY

HOW THE SIX ELEMENT SCORES ARE CALCULATED

Each animation is sampled across its full playable duration. Positions are measured relative to the hips, rotations use world-space quaternions, and central differences estimate velocity and acceleration. Scores describe this movement relative to its own activity range; they are analytical proxies, not absolute scientific or physiological measurements.
